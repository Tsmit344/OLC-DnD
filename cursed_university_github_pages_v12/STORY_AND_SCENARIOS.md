# Cursed University — Story & Scenario Bank

Game content: **6 characters × 4 years × 3 scenarios = 72 character-specific scenarios**. Years 1–2 share course types but feature different perspectives; Years 3–4 are specific to each major.

## Welcome story

At Cursed University, the Chancellor of Shortcuts has learned how to turn every assignment into a polished submission and every lesson into a forgotten echo. The great Brain of Understanding, sealed in a glass dome, has nearly gone dark. Your table becomes a party of instructional designers guiding one student from freshman year to commencement. Protect meaningful thinking, give students a reason to engage, and make the conditions of learning reasonable.

Every scenario asks the table to propose (1) purposeful AI friction and (2) at least one redesign element. Scenario answers are formative preparation. Each year ends with a scored boss defense; graduation is the final scored defense.

## Four-year structure

- **Year 1: The Hall of Beginnings** — General education • discovering why learning matters
- **Year 2: The Library of Echoes** — General education • evidence, ethics, and collaboration
- **Year 3: The Tower of Trials** — Major courses • making real disciplinary decisions
- **Year 4: The Hall of Ascension** — Major courses • demonstrating readiness

## Scoring and completion

Four year-end boss defenses (20 KP each) + one graduation defense (20 KP) = **100 KP maximum**. Each scored response gets 0 or 10 for AI friction plus 0 or 10 for at least one correctly applied redesign element. Facilitators confirm scores. If a party misses points, it may revise and resubmit a defense; only the improvement is added. A team graduates only after finishing four years and earning all 100 KP.

## Boss format

After each three-scenario set, roll a virtual d20 or enter a physical d20 result. The roll assigns a challenge category rather than points. The final fight at Commencement asks the team to justify one cohesive assessment-design decision, its limitations, and what the character has learned.

## Facilitation notes

Keep the AI-friction criterion focused on preserving learning—not AI bans for their own sake. Award a framework point only when one of the three elements is applied specifically to the assessment. Scenarios can be discussed collaboratively; typed responses are saved on the shared device. No AI inference occurs in this static release.
